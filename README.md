## 🎯 Project Objective

The goal of this dashboard is to:

- Track patient volume and admission trends  
- Analyze treatment costs by department and doctor  
- Monitor length of hospital stay  
- Understand demographic distribution of patients  

This dashboard supports data-driven decisions related to cost control, staffing, and resource allocation.

---

## 📊 Key Features

- KPI cards showing total patients, maximum & minimum treatment cost, average length of stay, and total treatment cost  
- Department-wise patient count and treatment cost analysis  
- Doctor-wise workload and cost contribution  
- Monthly admission trend visualization  
- Gender distribution of patients  
- Interactive slicers for department, doctor, gender, and year  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop  
- Power Query for data cleaning  
- DAX for calculated measures  
- Microsoft Excel (data source)

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

The dataset includes:

- Patient ID  
- Department  
- Doctor Name  
- Gender  
- Admission Date  
- Discharge Date  
- Length of Stay (Days)  
- Treatment Cost  

---

## 💡 Business Insights

- Identifies departments with high patient load and high cost  
- Highlights doctors handling larger case volumes  
- Detects patterns between length of stay and treatment cost  
- Helps management optimize hospital resources
